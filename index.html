OpenIT

We are an IT consultation service for nonprofits and small-owned businesses. NeighborhoodNerd21 started with one nerd who wanted to help those that help others. We hope to grow into a network of other like-minded nerds with a vision to change the way today's tech world works.

The tech ecosystem today is overwhelming and difficult to navigate for non-technical individuals. Tech-debt can bury your nonprofit or small business under subscriptions and lock-ins. With OpenIT, our goal is to foster transparency and collaboration between organizations, while providing low- or no-cost assistance and training to non-profits and small businesses. By doing so, we can actively reduce barriers between the people trying to make a difference and the tools they need to succeed.
