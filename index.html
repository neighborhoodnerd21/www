Open IT

We are an IT consultation service for nonprofits and small-owned businesses. NeighborhoodNerd21 started with one nerd who wanted to help those that help others. We hope to grow into a network of other like-minded nerds with a vision to change the way today's tech world works by applying some of the same philosophy and concepts used in Open Source Software. The strength of the Open IT vision finds roots in three core values: community, opportunity and integrity.

Community

Opportunity

Integrity

The tech ecosystem today is overwhelming and difficult to navigate for non-technical individuals. Tech-debt can bury your nonprofit or small business under subscriptions and lock-ins. Uninformed users with poor internet habits can lead to security issues and data loss. With Open IT, our goal is to foster transparency and collaboration between organizations, while providing low- or no-cost assistance and training to non-profits and small businesses. By doing so, we can actively reduce barriers between the people trying to make a difference and the tools they need to succeed.
