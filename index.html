_OpenIT Consultation

We are an IT consultation service for nonprofits and small-owned businesses. The tech ecosystem of our time can be overwhelming and difficult to navigate for non-technical individuals. Tech-debt can bury your nonprofit or small business under subscriptions and lock-ins. We would like to help. NeighborhoodNerd21 started with one nerd who wanted to help those that help others and hopes to grow into a network of other like-minded nerds with a vision to change the way to tech world works.
