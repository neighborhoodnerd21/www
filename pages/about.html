NeighborhoodNerd21

We are an IT consultation service for nonprofits and small-owned businesses. NeighborhoodNerd21 started with one nerd who wanted to help those that help others. We hope to grow into a network of other like-minded nerds with a vision to change the way today's tech world works by applying some of the same philosophy and concepts used in Open Source Software. The strength of the Open IT vision finds roots in three core values: community, opportunity and integrity.

The tech ecosystem is overwhelming and difficult to navigate for non-technical individuals. Tech-debt can bury your nonprofit or small business under subscriptions and lock-ins. Uninformed users with poor internet habits can lead to security issues and data loss. With Open IT, our goal is to foster transparency and collaboration between organizations, while providing low- or no-cost assistance and training to non-profits and small businesses. By doing so, we can actively reduce barriers between the people trying to make a difference and the tools they need to succeed.

As we are actively developing this platform, we are still growing our knowledge base and might not have every solution immediately available. If we cannot solve your problem directly, we will gladly help you find the right resources or connect you with someone who can. We are also looking for people to join our mission! If you love free and open-source software, enjoy helping others, and want to be part of our community, please get in touch. There are countless ways to contribute.

Need assistance or have questions?

If you are a non-profit or small business looking for support, please reach out to us. Answers are completely free, with no fees or dues required.
